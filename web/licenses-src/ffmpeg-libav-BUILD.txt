FFmpeg audio decoder (libav.js build) used by this website
==========================================================

Some files your browser cannot read itself (for example WMA, AVI, FLV, MPEG,
AC-3, DTS, AMR, WavPack) are decoded in your browser by FFmpeg, compiled to
WebAssembly with libav.js. FFmpeg and libav.js are licensed under the GNU
Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later (full text alongside this
file). The build is LGPL-only: no GPL, non-free or version-3 parts, decoders
and demuxers only. We did not modify FFmpeg or libav.js; we only chose which
components to build.

Source code
-----------
FFmpeg 9.0:   https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.tar.xz
              (sha256 7f607a00dd0d28a729d5a4811205812eef01cf6ef6155025febb6f36a9062d52)
libav.js:     https://github.com/Yahweasel/libav.js  tag v6.10.9.0
              (commit c80e885c3461f7bb7ea565c9631b34243ae0dbf1)
Emscripten:   emsdk 6.0.5
zlib 1.3.2:   https://www.zlib.net/  (zlib licence)
emfiberthreads 1.3: https://github.com/Yahweasel/emfiberthreads (0BSD licence)

You may replace the decoder with your own build: it is the three files under
/dfn/libav/<version>/ that the page loads.

Written offer: for at least three years after we last served this build, we
will send anyone who asks, through the contact form on the website (the Contact
page; choose "A question"), the
complete corresponding source code (FFmpeg, libav.js and their dependencies as
listed above) together with the exact build script we used, for no more than
the cost of sending it.

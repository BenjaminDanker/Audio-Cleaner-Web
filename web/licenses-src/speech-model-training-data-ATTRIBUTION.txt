Training data of the downloaded models: attribution
====================================================

Audio Cleaner does not contain or distribute any of this data. The models it downloads learned from the data sets
below, which are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are named here as that licence asks.

AP-BWE (the model that restores missing highs, see AP-BWE-MIT.txt)
  VCTK Corpus 0.92: Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald, "CSTR VCTK Corpus: English
  Multi-speaker Corpus for CSTR Voice Cloning Toolkit (version 0.92)", The Centre for Speech Technology Research
  (CSTR), University of Edinburgh, 2019. https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645

Sidon (the model that rebuilds the voice, see Sidon-MIT.txt)
  FLEURS-R (huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs-r): Min Ma, Yuma Koizumi, Shigeki Karita, Heiga Zen, Jason Riesa,
  Haruko Ishikawa and Michiel Bacchiani, "FLEURS-R: A Restored Multilingual Speech Corpus for Generation Tasks",
  Interspeech 2024. Google.
  LibriTTS-R (huggingface.co/datasets/parler-tts/libritts_r_filtered, a filtered copy): Yuma Koizumi, Heiga Zen,
  Shigeki Karita, Yifan Ding, Kohei Yatabe, Nobuyuki Morioka, Michiel Bacchiani, Yu Zhang, Wei Han and Ankur
  Bapna, "LibriTTS-R: A Restored Multi-Speaker Text-to-Speech Corpus", Interspeech 2023. Google.

The data sets were used by the model authors to train the models; the models are separate works under the MIT
licences in this folder. No endorsement by the authors of the data sets is implied.

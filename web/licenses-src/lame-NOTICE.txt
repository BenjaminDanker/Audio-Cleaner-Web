LAME MP3 encoder (inside @mediabunny/mp3-encoder) used by this website
=======================================================================

When you save a result as an MP3, your browser downloads one small extra file
(a script with the MP3 encoder inside it) and writes the MP3 itself, on your
own device. Nothing is uploaded. That encoder is LAME, compiled to WebAssembly
by the mediabunny project (@mediabunny/mp3-encoder, MPL-2.0).

LAME is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2 or
later (the full text of version 2.1 is alongside this file). We did not modify
LAME or the encoder package. The encoder lives in its own file, separate from
the rest of the site, so it can be replaced by a modified copy of LAME.

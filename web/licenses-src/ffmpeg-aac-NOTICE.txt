FFmpeg's AAC encoder (inside @mediabunny/aac-encoder) used by this website
=========================================================================

When your browser writes AAC sound (an M4A file, or the sound of an MP4, MOV
or MKV video), it downloads one extra file the first time (a script with the
AAC encoder inside it) and writes the sound itself, on your own device.
Nothing is uploaded. That encoder is FFmpeg's AAC encoder (libavcodec 62.23,
nothing else of FFmpeg), compiled to WebAssembly by the mediabunny project
(@mediabunny/aac-encoder, MPL-2.0).

FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1
or later (the full text is alongside this file). We did not modify FFmpeg or
the encoder package. The encoder lives in its own file, separate from the
rest of the site, so it can be replaced by a modified copy of FFmpeg.
